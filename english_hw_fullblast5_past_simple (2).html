📖 Grammar Reference
✅ Affirmative

Was / Were

I / He / She / It → WAS She was happy.
You / We / They → WERE They were tired.
❌ Negative

Wasn't / Weren't

He → wasn't He wasn't scared.
We → weren't We weren't ready.
❓ Question

Was / Were + subject

Was + I/he/she/it…? Was it fun?
Were + you/we/they…? Were they late?
📝 Rules for adding -ED to Regular Verbs

How to form the Past Simple

Rule 1 · Most verbs
+ -ed
walk → walked
visit → visited
Rule 2 · Ends in -e
+ -d only
love → loved
arrive → arrived
Rule 3 · CVC short
Double + -ed
stop → stopped
plan → planned
Rule 4 · Ends in -y
y → i + -ed
study → studied
carry → carried
Negat. form
didn't + verb
She didn't walk.
They didn't stop.
Question form
Did + sub + verb?
Did he arrive?
Did they study?
