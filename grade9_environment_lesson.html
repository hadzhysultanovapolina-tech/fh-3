🎧 Activity 4 — Listening Comprehension
9
Listening · Read & answer

📖 Read the text, then answer the comprehension questions below.

🌍 Our Planet Is In Danger

Every year, millions of tonnes of rubbish are thrown into the oceans. Factories release dangerous pollution into the air and water. As a result, many species of animals and plants are endangered — they may disappear forever.

Global warming is making our climate more extreme. Polar ice is melting, and sea levels are rising. Forests are being destroyed by fire and illegal cutting. If we do not act now, the damage to our environment will be impossible to repair.

But there is hope. People all over the world are working to protect wildlife, reduce pollution and recycle waste. If every person becomes more responsible, we can save our planet for future generations.

1. What happens to many species because of pollution?

2. What is causing sea levels to rise?

3. According to the text, what can save our planet?
