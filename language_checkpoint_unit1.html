⚡ Present Perfect Simple vs Continuous

Present Perfect Simple
Experience: I've seen that film.
Recent result: She's lost her bag.
Just finished: They've just had an accident.
How many times: I've seen it three times.
Present Perfect Continuous
Duration/process: I've been studying for 2 hrs.
Recently finished: I've been washing dishes.
NOT for short actions: I've broken the window. ✓
with: for, since, how long, all day

🔤 Gerund (-ing) vs Infinitive (to + verb)

Use GERUND (-ing) after:
prepositions: interested in studying
like / love / enjoy / hate: I enjoy watching TV
go + activity: go swimming / running
admit / avoid / suggest: I suggest reading
Use INFINITIVE (to+verb) after:
want / hope / decide / need: I want to work
adjectives: It's good to revise
too / enough: It's too cold to go out
to explain why: He went to study
