📌 Grammar: Comparatives & Superlatives

⚡ Rules

➕ Comparative
heavy → heavier
intelligent → more intelligent
easy → easier
boring → more boring
old → older
young → younger
popular → more popular
🏆 Superlative
heavy → the heaviest
intelligent → the most intelligent
easy → the easiest
boring → the most boring
old → the oldest
young → the youngest
popular → the most popular
⭐ Irregular
good → better → the best
bad → worse → the worst
weigh = важити
A gorilla weighs more than a panda.
